## The beginning of something bigger

Sub Arctic Studios is an independent game development studio with a long horizon and an uncompromising interest in how games work—and how they make people feel.

We're here to create worlds players can learn, explore, compete in, laugh in, and remember. Worlds where individual mechanics connect to something greater: where decisions carry weight, cooperation creates opportunity, and the unexpected becomes part of the story.

We're at the beginning of that journey. We don't claim a catalog of finished games. We believe the work we do now—studying, documenting, experimenting, correcting, and improving—is how a lasting studio gets built.

> **Consistency compounds. Research informs. Experience teaches. Every iteration moves us forward.**

## Our flagship: WAR BLOX

**WAR BLOX** is our foundational multiplayer game project, being designed for Roblox.

Our vision is a large-scale, faction-based military sandbox that brings together responsive infantry combat, combined-arms vehicles, logistics, engineering, player-built forward operating bases, and a meaningful equipment economy.

The ambition isn't complexity for its own sake. It's **interconnection**: a supply pilot's delivery can sustain a defense; a builder's fortification can change a battle; a medic's rescue can save a teammate's hard-earned equipment. Different playstyles should have meaningful ways to shape the same living conflict.

**Current stage:** research-led preproduction, system architecture, and planning toward focused playable prototypes. Gameplay features discussed here are design goals, not claims of shipped functionality.

## How we build

We believe creative ambition deserves engineering discipline. Our studio workflow is being built around six habits:

| Principle | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| **Study deeply** | Research mechanics, player behavior, technical constraints, and existing solutions before making big commitments. |
| **Decide deliberately** | Separate observations and recommendations from decisions formally approved for the project. |
| **Document clearly** | Maintain a navigable source of truth, stable references, revision history, and linked design rationale. |
| **Build modularly** | Favor maintainable systems, clear ownership, reusable components, and secure multiplayer foundations. |
| **Verify honestly** | Review changes, test what can actually be tested, and never confuse a proposal with a working feature. |
| **Keep evolving** | Correct mistakes, revisit assumptions, improve the architecture, and carry lessons into the next project. |

Our documentation, version-control, and review practices are part of the product—not an afterthought. We're developing them to scale with the studio, to support human collaborators and AI-assisted workflows, and to preserve the reasoning behind what we build.
